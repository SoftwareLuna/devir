Help centre / Delete account

Delete your account and data

Delete your Anahtar account in the app or start a deletion request here if you cannot access the app.

Request deletion without opening the app

Email us from the address registered to your account and ask to delete your Anahtar account. If you no longer have access to that address, explain this in your message and provide a way for us to reply. We may contact you to verify account ownership. Please leave out passwords and identity documents.

Request account deletion ↗If the email button does not open, you can write directly to serdengecti.emre@gmail.com.

If you have an Apple subscription

Deleting your Anahtar account leaves Apple’s automatic renewal in place. To stop renewal, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions in your Apple Account, select the subscription and cancel it. You can delete your account before the subscription period ends. Deleting your account removes the link between your subscription and your account on Anahtar’s server. An Apple subscription does not automatically transfer to another Anahtar account.

Manage Apple subscriptions

Delete your account in the app

  1. Sign in to Anahtar. If you want to keep a copy of your records, use Settings → Prepare backup first and save the file somewhere secure.
  2. If you own a company workspace, open Settings → Team and invitations and select Remove from team for each other member.
  3. Go to Settings → Cloud and team → Delete account. Enter your current password and tap Permanently delete account.
  4. Confirm with Delete my account. Account deletion cannot be undone.

If you have forgotten your password, choose Forgot password on the sign-in screen.

What will be deleted?

  • Member or standalone account. Your cloud account and any team membership are deleted. The company’s shared rental records and activity history remain. That history may include your account identifier or email address.
  • Company owner. Deleting your account also deletes the company’s cloud workspace, stored versions, documents, invitations and activity history. Removing other team members does not delete their individual sign-in accounts.
  • Records on devices. If the device where you delete your account has that cloud workspace open, its saved copy and the in-app recovery backups are cleared. If a separate local workspace is open, its records are kept. A request sent by email does not give us remote access to local records on your devices. Manage exported backups and shared PDFs separately. Provider logs and backups follow the provider’s own deletion processes.

There is no automatic expiry period for the company’s activity history or earlier workspace versions.

Local-only use or rental customer records

If you never created an account, you have no Anahtar cloud account to delete. To remove your local workspace from the device, choose Delete App in iOS. Offloading the app keeps its documents. Manage device backups and copies saved to Files separately.

If you rented a vehicle from a company and want your rental record deleted, contact the business that entered your information.
