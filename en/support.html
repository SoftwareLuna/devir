Frequently asked questions

Open settings from the gear in the top right of Overview on iPhone, or from Company in the iPad sidebar.

Can I use Devir without an account?

Yes. Choose Start without an account when you first open Devir. Your records stay on that device, and you can work offline.

To access your company from another device, go to Settings → Create account / sign in. Once signed in, choose Connect this company to cloud to share your local company. You can invite colleagues under Team and invitations.

How do I move my records and documents to a new device?

If you use Devir locally, open Settings → Prepare backup on the old device. Choose Share backup / Save to Files and save the file somewhere secure. On the new device, open Settings → Restore backup and select the file.

Restoring replaces the records on that device. If the new device already has records you want to keep, back them up first. If it is connected to a cloud company, sign out of Devir before restoring.

How do I keep track of upcoming pickups and returns?

Open Calendar and tap a day to see its rentals. Choose Fleet schedule to compare bookings across vehicles.

On each device where you want a reminder, open Settings → Notifications. Allow notifications, turn on Reminders and choose pickups, returns or payments. Reminders normally arrive one hour before the scheduled time. Open Devir on that device to pick up schedule changes made by your team.

Why won’t the camera open to scan a licence or registration?

While editing the record, choose the document type and tap Scan document. If camera access is off, tap Open Settings in the alert and allow camera access for Devir. If you already have a PDF, choose Add from Files in the same section.

How do I create and share a signed rental agreement?

Open the rental and choose Agreements → Prepare agreement. Select a template, then tap Create and review PDF. After reviewing the document, tap Sign, enter the signer’s name and have them sign on screen. Save the signed PDF.

Reopen the agreement from the rental. Choose Share / Print from the menu in the upper right.
